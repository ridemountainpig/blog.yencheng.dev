We've all been there: checking our credit card statement only to find a charge for a service we haven't used in months. Or realizing too late that a "free trial" had quietly converted into a paid annual plan.

In today's digital life, subscriptions are everywhere. Netflix, Spotify, SaaS tools, cloud storage... the list goes on. It's becoming increasingly difficult to keep a mental inventory of what we're paying for and when.

That's why I built [Subflow](https://subflow.ing/en).

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-1.png)

[Subflow](https://subflow.ing/en) is a simple, powerful tool designed to bring sanity back to your recurring expenses. It helps you organize everything in one place, so you never have to worry about surprise charges again.

## Why Subflow?

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-2.png)

The goal of Subflow is simple: clarity. When your subscriptions are scattered across different emails and credit card statements, it's easy to lose track. Subflow gives you a centralized dashboard to see exactly where your money is going.

## Key Features

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-3.png)

### ✨ Free Forever:

No pricing tiers. No paywalls. Subflow is completely free to use. Manage your subscriptions without adding another one to the list.

### 🤖 Smart Add Subscription:

![Subflow](https://subflow.ing/videos/smart-add-demo.mp4)

Quickly add subscriptions with intelligent auto-detection. Paste in subscription details, email receipt, or description to automatically extract subscription details. Streamlined input with smart suggestions and autofill makes managing your subscriptions effortless.

### 📦 SVGL Integration:

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-4.png)

Start quickly with built-in services powered by [SVGL](https://svgl.app/) icons. Subflow's library makes it easy to recognize your services at a glance.

### 🔄 Always in Sync:

Your subscription data is always up to date wherever you log in. Secure and real-time synchronization ensures you have access to your dashboard from any device.

### 💱 Multi-Currency Support:

Track your subscriptions in any currency that matters to you. Whether it's USD, TWD, or JPY, Subflow handles the conversion automatically so you can see your total spending in your preferred currency.

### ✉️ Email Notification:

Never miss a payment again. Subflow's email notification feature reminds you before your subscription payment is due and notifies you of exactly what you'll be paying for.

### 👥 Co-Subscriptions:

Share subscriptions with family and friends so you can manage expenses together. Easily track who is paying for what and split costs without the headache.

### 📅 Calendar View:

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-5.png)

Visualize your upcoming charges on a dedicated calendar. See exactly when payments are due and get a clear picture of your billing cycles.

### 📊 Visual Analytics:

![Subflow](/blog/introducing-subflow/subflow-6.png)

Understand your spending habits with interactive charts. Subflow breaks down your expenses, showing you spending ratios and monthly totals.
