![Bubble tea cups and Raycast stickers lined up before the event](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-01-BZE3-gvQ.jpg)

Before the event started, cups of bubble tea were already lined up on the table.

On the afternoon of May 30, 19 Raycast fans walked into our venue in Taichung. The first ever `Rayboba` was about to begin: Raycast plus bubble tea 🧋.

[Yiwei](https://x.com/1weiho) and I hosted this event together. Our plan was simple: a Raycast meetup that belonged to Taiwan. What we didn't expect was that, over one afternoon, the whole Raycast world would squeeze into that one room.

Right at the start, everyone grabbed their bubble tea and we took a group photo together.

![Rayboba Taichung group photo](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-13-_4uLngnf.jpg)

## Starting from Taichung: What the Taiwan Community Is Working On

We opened by introducing what the `Raycast Taiwan Community` has been up to: what the Taiwan ambassadors are working on, the content on our [Threads](https://www.threads.net/@raycast.tw) and [community website](https://www.raycast.tw), and why we wanted to host this meetup in the first place.

![Introducing the Raycast Taiwan ambassadors](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-14-CHGq2ZT3.jpg)

## Greetings from Europe: A Video from the Raycast Team

The Raycast team couldn't fly to Taichung in person, but they showed up in their own way.

[Thomas](https://x.com/thomaspaulmann) and [Pedro](https://x.com/peduarte) recorded a video just for Rayboba, and we added Chinese subtitles to it. The moment we hit play, the room went quiet: the Raycast team was right there on the other side of the screen, greeting the Taiwan community.

There was one lovely detail in the video: Thomas and Pedro were holding bubble tea too, toasting the room in Taichung through the screen.

![Thomas and Pedro greeting the Taiwan community with bubble tea in hand](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-19-DFYo3RGf.jpg)

## Friends Across the Sea: Stories from the Japan Community

Next to show up was the [Raycast Japan Community](https://x.com/Raycast0731), from across the sea.

We shared photos from their events, the content they have been creating, and a preview of their upcoming Tokyo events in August and September. In another country, a group of people is doing exactly what we are doing: turning a tool they love into a community.

## Raycast 2.0: Built Far Away, Right There in the Room

The second session was all about `Raycast 2.0`. We started with an installation walkthrough, went through [What's new in 2.0](https://manual.raycast.com/new-in-v2), and introduced the new architecture behind it.

Some attendees were already on 2.0, and everyone loved the update. Yiwei also shared behind-the-scenes insights into how the Raycast team built the new Raycast, showing that this release is not just a new interface but a redesign of the entire architecture.

![Raycast 2.0 session, bubble tea on every table](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-07-BGh-UfFo.jpg)

## Building an Extension from Scratch

The Raycast world isn't just something you watch. You can build a part of it yourself, and this was the session I looked forward to the most.

We first introduced the Raycast extension ecosystem: the Store, how installation works, and AI Extensions, along with the four command types: Commands, Menubar, AI Commands, and Background Scripts.

Then came the live demo: starting from the `Create Extension` command, we built a Svgl extension step by step, wired up the Raycast Extension Skill, developed it with the Extension Development command, and published it to the Team Store.

We also talked about contributing to extensions: every extension is open source, and anyone can become a contributor, from reporting an issue to forking a repo and opening their first Pull Request with the `PR` command.

![Extension development live demo](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-09-B075JZIC.jpg)

## The First Look at Glaze

After a short break, the final session belonged to something that hadn't even officially launched yet: `Glaze`, the new product from the Raycast team.

We introduced what Glaze is, how to get beta access, and gave a live demo of the Glaze app. Everyone was surprised by Glaze and loved trying it.

![Glaze introduction and demo](https://www.raycast.tw/assets/rayboba-taichung-05-30-17-BBWhqMYU.jpg)

## After the Bubble Tea

And just like that, the first ever Rayboba came to an end.

Looking back on that afternoon: the room held all of us in Taichung, the Raycast team on the screen, the Japan community from across the sea, and the latest 2.0 and Glaze. A local meetup, yet it felt like the whole Raycast world was there.

Thank you to [Thomas](https://x.com/thomaspaulmann) and [Pedro](https://x.com/peduarte) for the video, to Stephanie for her help, to Yiwei for co-hosting with me, and to every single person who joined us.

One day, I hope the friends on the screen and across the sea will visit Taiwan in person and share a bubble tea with us. 🧋

This was only the first Rayboba. See you at the next one.
