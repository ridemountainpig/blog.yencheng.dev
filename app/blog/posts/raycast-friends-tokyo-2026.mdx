Last year, I visited Tokyo as a Raycast Ambassador from Taiwan and joined a Raycast community event. There, for the first time, I met many friends I had previously known only through X.

When I returned in August 2026, friends reached out before the trip to ask when I was free. I had not expected the people I met at one event to become close international friends.

## Where It Started: From X to Tokyo

In 2025, I became a `Raycast Ambassador` representing the community in Taiwan and co-hosted `Raycast Cafe Taichung`. Through X, I also got to know members of the [Raycast Japan Community](https://x.com/Raycast0731), including [Yano](https://x.com/nagauta_jp), [Seiji](https://x.com/se_eiji), and many others.

Later that year, [Yiwei](https://x.com/1weiho) and I traveled to Tokyo as Raycast Ambassadors from Taiwan to join the `Tokyo AI Hackathon` and `Raycast Meetup Japan #2`. It was my first international community event in this role and my first time meeting many of these online friends in person.

I shared more about that trip in [My Raycast Adventure in 2025](/blog/raycast-adventure-2025). We continued talking and following each other's work after the event.

## Back in Tokyo, Meeting Friends Again

Before I returned to Tokyo, people I had met through Raycast asked about my schedule and whether we could meet. Some of them had already been waiting for another opportunity to get together.

The community had a `Tokyo Raycafe` planned for September 1, but unfortunately I left Japan before then and could not join. Even so, Yano invited me to two morning Raycafes of our own, a `Raycast Taiwan Community` X `Raycast Japan Community` crossover.

At the first Raycafe, we sat in a coffee shop and talked about the Raycast communities in Taiwan and Japan, what we had each been working on lately, and our favorite Raycast features. Yano later [shared some photos from that morning](https://x.com/nagauta_jp/status/2090623191618322714) on X.

![Yano and Yen at a morning Raycafe in Tokyo](/blog/raycast-friends-tokyo-2026/raycafe-2.webp)

A few days later, Yano invited me to [another morning Raycafe](https://x.com/nagauta_jp/status/2092073789446328824).

![Coffee at another morning Raycafe](/blog/raycast-friends-tokyo-2026/raycafe-3.webp)

## Keeping in Touch After the Event

After the first Tokyo event, we kept in touch through X and the Raycast community. We followed what each other was building and shared updates about the communities in Taiwan and Japan.

During this visit, friends wanted to meet again, and those who could not make it sent me messages. This was when I truly realized how many friends from different countries the Raycast community had brought into my life.

At first, Raycast was just a productivity tool to me. Later, I started contributing extensions, became an Ambassador, and joined community events in Taiwan and abroad.

I thought most of the conversations along the way would be about commands, extensions, and product ideas. What Raycast brought me in the end was a group of close friends from different countries.

## Looking Forward to a Raycast Asia Tour

One of Yano's dreams is to create a `Raycast Asia Tour` and connect Raycast communities across different Asian countries.

I am also excited about this dream and look forward to seeing it happen. I hope it gives more community members the opportunity to meet in person.

Thank you to Yano and everyone who made time to meet me or sent me a message during this visit.

I hope Taiwan can one day be a stop on Yano's Raycast Asia Tour. I would love to welcome Yano and the Raycast Japan Community here, and I look forward to seeing that day come.
